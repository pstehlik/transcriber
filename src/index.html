Transcriber

Welcome! A one-time setup is needed before you can start transcribing.

This will download and install:
• MLX Whisper (speech recognition engine)
• Language model (~1.5 GB)
• ffmpeg (audio processing, if not already installed)

Depending on your internet speed, this takes 2–5 minutes.
All processing stays on your Mac — nothing is uploaded.

Requires Apple Silicon (M1+) and macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
